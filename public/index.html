    Why HuggingFound

    The models are free. The only bill is the machine.

    A paid assistant is $20 a month and rising, and its maker still loses money on every answer. Here the models cost nothing. Small ones run on your laptop, and a GPU for the big ones is about $0.20 an hour, stopped the moment you are done.

    • No subscription and no tokens to buy
    • A GPU from $0.20 an hour, under a cap you set
    • Hundreds of thousands of models, yours to keep

    Why open models

    AI tokens won't be cheap forever. Explore open-source models today

    Every answer from a paid assistant costs its maker more than you pay for it. Investors cover the gap for now.

    $115 billion

    OpenAI's expected cash burn through 2029

    Reuters, Sep 2025

    $8.7 billion

    Spent by OpenAI on inference in nine months, against $4.3 billion of revenue

    The Register, Nov 2025

    $14 billion

    OpenAI's projected loss for 2026

    R&D World, Jan 2026

    $9 billion

    Anthropic's compute bill for the first half of 2026

    Yahoo Finance, Oct 2026

    Someone pays that back, and it will be the user. One chatbot user in twenty pays today, and $20 a month is "not a sustainable price" (The Boston Globe, April 2026). Open models cost nothing; the only bill is for the computer or the rented GPU they run on, and you set the cap.

    How it fits together

    A day's work, on open models

    Each model hands its result to the next. Nothing is paid for the models.

    1. Hugging Face, a million open models.
    2. Notes model turns the meeting into notes.
    3. Coding model builds what the notes ask for.
    4. Video model makes the launch clip.

    Where it runs

    Small models on your computer. Heavier ones on a GPU by the hour. A paid model you already have can check the work.

    1. HuggingFound finds the model and sets it up.
    2. Your computer runs small models; nothing leaves it.
    3. A rented GPU runs heavier ones by the hour, and stops itself.
    4. Your paid model, optional: checks the work; a free tier will do.

    Why HuggingFound

    Why use HuggingFound?

    When the service everyone relies on goes down

    One morning the assistant stops answering, the status page says nothing, and the other big services are down or full. HuggingFound is not one service. It opens the whole of Hugging Face, and the model you pick runs on your own computer or on a GPU rented by the minute. Nobody can switch it off.

    Every kind of model in one place

    Chat, code, math, pictures, video with sound, speech to text.

    Runs where you choose

    A laptop, or a rented card that stops itself and stays under your cap.

    Know a model before you fetch it

    What people say about it, summed up in a line on every card.

    Yours to keep

    Open weights do not expire. What you make stays on your computer.
